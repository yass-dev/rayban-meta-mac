To make it work

  • Glasses out of the case, arms open, next to the Mac.
  • During an import, the Mac spends a few minutes on the glasses' Wi-Fi and loses internet. It goes back to your Wi-Fi by itself.
  • If the connection does not go through: turn off Bluetooth on your phone, put the glasses in pairing mode (hold the capture button until the light blinks blue), then click Connect.

Account and pairing

Owner

Meta login

Only needed to pair again. Importing and control never need Meta. If Meta refuses the login: double-click Install.command in the project folder, then More options > Log in to Meta again.

Pair again

Makes this Mac the owner of the glasses. Only do this after resetting the glasses, or if they no longer recognize the Mac. The Meta AI app on your phone loses the glasses until you set them up there again.

  1. Glasses in pairing mode: hold the capture button until the light blinks blue.
  2. Bluetooth off on your phone.
  3. Type PAIR, then start. Accept the macOS “Connect” window if it appears.
